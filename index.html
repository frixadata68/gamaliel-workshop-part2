Why This Workshop

The Ground Is Shifting Under Engineering Careers

This is not a talk you sit through. It’s a ninety-minute working session built for engineers who are carrying real weight right now — and it treats resilience the way we’d treat any other system property: something you architect before the load arrives, not something you improvise during the incident.

Lew Cirne spent four decades building software that tells engineers what’s actually happening inside their systems. Here he turns that same instinct inward, teaching from three passages — Matthew 7:24–27, Hebrews 12:5–11, and Luke 22:31–32 — and pairing each with a hands-on lab. You’ll run a foundation audit, write a blameless postmortem of your last setback, query Scripture live with Gamaliel.ai, and leave with a 30-day runbook.

Why now
  • Re-scoped roles — budgets are moving toward AI, and teams are being reshaped around it.
  • Real pressure — mortgages, loans, and families don’t pause while you search.
  • Faster cycles — the stack you mastered can feel obsolete in a single release cycle.

Systems fail. Markets turn. The question is what you’re built on.

Your Guest Speaker

Lew Cirne

Lew Cirne
Founder & Chairman, The Gamaliel Project  ·  Founder, New Relic & Wily Technology

Lew Cirne is the entrepreneur who invented the Application Performance Management category. He founded Wily Technology in 1998, pioneering the bytecode instrumentation agent that became the foundation of the entire APM market; CA Technologies acquired Wily in 2006 for $375 million. He then founded New Relic in 2008 — named as an anagram of his own name — scaling it from a prototype he hand-coded in Ruby to a public company taken private in 2023 for approximately $6.5 billion.

Known throughout Silicon Valley as the “Coding CEO,” Lew dedicated two full days every week to writing code even while running a public company, and holds 19 patents in application performance management. In 2024 he was inducted into the Dartmouth College Entrepreneurs Hall of Fame.

He also built Gamaliel.ai, the AI Bible reader at the center of this workshop’s middle layer — named for the rabbi who mentored the Apostle Paul, free on web, iOS and Android, with its prompts and theological guardrails MIT-licensed on GitHub. Lew returns to The Pivot Point Network not to demo a product, but to teach what he has learned about what holds when the storm actually hits.

Before You Attend

How to Prepare

Bring a laptop or phone. Two of the four labs are hands-on — you’ll be working in the tool, not watching a demo.
Open Gamaliel.ai once beforehand so you’re not setting up during Lab 3. It’s free, and an account is optional — the iOS and Android apps work too.
Read the three passages ahead of time: Matthew 7:24–27, Hebrews 12:5–11, and Luke 22:31–32.
Come ready to think about one real setback — a layoff, a failed interview loop, a missed promotion. Lab 2 is a postmortem of it, written privately.
Come with 3 thoughtful, insightful questions prepared for Lew — the more specific, the better.
Review Lew’s LinkedIn profile to familiarize yourself with his background and career journey ahead of the workshop.
